About the conference

A clear map of ideas, practice and cooperation.

CEEDUCON brings together people working with internationalisation of higher education. The programme reflects the main questions of the field: responsible digital change, inclusive international experiences, sustainable partnerships and future skills.

AI and digitalisation inclusive mobility global partnerships alumni and careers

Thematic tracks

Main thematic areas of the conference.

The structure follows the CEEDUCON themes and helps participants quickly understand which sessions connect to their work.

01

Navigating the Technological Shift

Responsible use of AI, digitalisation and data analytics for smarter internationalisation strategies.

02

Challenges of Internationalisation

Structural, social and financial barriers, wellbeing, access and safe international experiences for all.

03

Global & Regional Partnerships

Sustainable partnerships, European University alliances and cooperation across global regions.

04

From Recruitment to Retention

A student-centred journey from recruitment and admissions to support, alumni and employability.
