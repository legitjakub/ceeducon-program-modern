About the conference

A one-day map of higher education internationalisation.

CEEDUCON is a platform for sharing practice, trends and strategic questions in international education. The programme is designed for fast orientation: time, room, thematic track and a personal selection.

AI and digitalisation inclusive mobility global partnerships alumni and careers

Thematic tracks

Four tracks that structure the whole day.

The tracks follow the CEEDUCON structure and help participants understand which sessions are most relevant to them.

01

Navigating the Technological Shift

AI, digitalisation and data as tools for smarter internationalisation while preserving academic values.

02

Challenges of Internationalisation

Structural, social and financial barriers, mobility safety, wellbeing and inclusion in practice.

03

Global & Regional Partnerships

Strategic partnerships, European University alliances and cooperation with global regions.

04

From Recruitment to Retention

The student journey from recruitment and admission to support, success, alumni and employability.
