Two days. One programme.

CEEDUCON 2026 runs across the halls of O2 universum Prague on 1–2 December. Browse the preliminary room-by-room programme below — sessions, workshops and speakers for both days.

Preliminary programme Online now

Registration opens in September and participation is free of charge. The programme remains subject to change.

Overview

How the two days are planned.

Both days run as full conference days with plenaries, thematic sessions and workshops — connected by a networking dinner on the first evening.

Day 1 · Tue 1 Dec

All-day conference

Opening plenary and thematic sessions across the four 2026 themes at O2 universum.

Evening

Networking dinner

An evening dedicated to informal exchange and new partnerships. Details will follow with the final programme.

Day 2 · Wed 2 Dec

All-day conference

A second full day of sessions and workshops, closing with a joint plenary.
