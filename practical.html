Essentials

Venue, access and travel basics.

The information is grouped by visitor intent so attendees can quickly answer: where do I go, how do I get there and what should I prepare?

Venue

O2 universum

Ceskomoravska 17, Prague 9. The conference venue hosts both CEEDUCON 2026 conference days.

From airport

Airport connection

Use trolleybus 59 to Nadrazi Veleslavin, then metro A and B towards Ceskomoravska. The journey takes around 55 minutes.

From rail

Main station and Liben

From the Main Train Station, use metro C and B. From Praha-Liben, walk about 10 minutes or use tram 7 or 8.

Access

English and accessibility

The conference language is English. The venue is accessible for visitors using a wheelchair.

Accommodation

Participants arrange accommodation individually. Nearby options listed by the official information include Stages Hotel and Carol Hotel.

Conference fee

There is no conference fee for registered attendees. Registration opens in September.

Transport updates

Check the venue website and current Prague public transport information before travelling, especially around Ceskomoravska metro access.
