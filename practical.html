Essentials

Venue, access and travel basics.

The conference language is English, participation is free of charge and the venue is fully accessible.

Venue

O2 universum

Českomoravská 17, Prague 9. All plenaries, sessions and workshops of CEEDUCON 2026 take place here.

From the airport

Around 55 minutes

Take trolleybus 59 to Nádraží Veleslavín, then metro line A and line B towards Českomoravská.

By train

Main station & Libeň

From the Main Train Station use metro lines C and B. From Praha-Libeň it is a 10-minute walk or a short ride on tram 7 or 8.

Accessibility

Accessible & in English

The conference is held in English and the venue is accessible for visitors using a wheelchair.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Is there a conference fee?

No — participation at CEEDUCON 2026 is free of charge for registered attendees. Registration opens in September.

Where should I stay?

Participants arrange accommodation individually. Hotels within easy reach of the venue include Stages Hotel and Carol Hotel; central Prague is around 20 minutes away by metro.

How do I register?

Registration opens in September. The registration form and practical details will be published on the official CEEDUCON website once confirmed.

Anything to check before travelling?

Check current Prague public transport information before you travel, especially around the Českomoravská metro station, and verify travel requirements for the Czech Republic if you need a visa or an event confirmation from the organisers.
