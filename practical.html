Venue

Českomoravská 17, Prague 9. All plenaries, sessions and workshops of CEEDUCON 2026 take place here.

Travel

By public transport

From the airport around 55 minutes by trolleybus 59 and metro. From the Main Train Station by metro to the Českomoravská station.

Inclusive

Accessible

The venue is fully accessible for wheelchair users.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Is there a conference fee?

No — participation at CEEDUCON 2026 is free of charge for registered attendees. Registration is open.

Is there a limit on the number of participants?

Yes. The conference venue has a limited capacity, and registration will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. We therefore encourage you to register as early as possible to secure your place. Once the conference reaches its maximum capacity, registration will be closed.

Where should I stay?

Participants are responsible for arranging and covering the cost of their own accommodation. Please note that neither accommodation nor travel expenses can be reimbursed by the conference organizers. Hotels within easy reach of the venue include Stages Hotel and Carol Hotel, while Prague city centre is approximately 20 minutes away by metro.

How do I register?

Registration is open. Places are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, so register early to secure yours.

Anything to check before travelling?

Check current Prague public transport information before you travel, especially around the Českomoravská metro station, and verify travel requirements for the Czech Republic if you need a visa or an event confirmation from the organisers.
